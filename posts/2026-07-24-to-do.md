I have been getting back into following my interest lately. Photography, motorcycle riding, reading and writing etc. As you grow older, it seems like there is less time to do things that you want. The list of things you want to do on the other hand, seems to be increasing. 

Putting life in perspective of time left to live is a good way to find some motivation to move things forward. Whether those are your interests, hobbies or things that you want to do.

It is going to be a busy year for me for various personal reasons, but I am looking forward to diving deep into some of my hobbies that I had put aside. Photography being primarily one of them.

Nothing tells your life's story like photographs. That is the way to remember life as it happened. Otherwise, we forget what we did.
