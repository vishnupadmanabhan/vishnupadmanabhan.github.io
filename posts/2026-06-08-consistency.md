This is a post that serves as a little note to myself. A reminder that I can do things. That I have to do things — make things. A reminder that I need to stay consistent and disciplined if I want to get anywhere close to the person I always wished to be. This is a reminder that time is ticking.

> Think back to when you were younger, your school or college days. There was a dream. There were things you wanted to do, and you hoped that a mature adult version of yourself would one day take care of all of it and live the life you had imagined. There was a promise made to yourself that motivated you as you grew up.
>
> It is time to fulfil that promise. To show that little kid that everything which was once a dream can become real.
>
> So make that thing you were procrastinating on. Go pick up that camera or that phone. Edit that video. Record that voiceover. Do the thing you have been putting off.
>
> You have to fulfil the promise.
>
> So do it, and don’t disappoint that younger version of yourself. Ever.

I will look back at this post every three months and recalibrate my path. That is a promise I make to myself today.
